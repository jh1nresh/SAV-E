# Home — Little Atlas prompt

Read `../design.md`. Reproduce the locked Home composition, not a generic
travel dashboard.

Build a 402 × 874 pt iOS screen where an illustrated Tokyo atlas is the dominant
surface. Use the unchanged Memo mascot with the SAV-E wordmark. Place one small
Paste a link capsule in the header. On the atlas, show hand-drawn roads, water,
landmarks, two saved pins, and a few restrained place labels. Memo must peek
from behind one paper review sheet anchored to the lower map edge. The sheet
contains `3 clues need your help`, one coral `Review clues` action, and Review /
Map Stamp counts. Under the map show one compact Tokyo Weekend row and exactly
two recent Map Stamps. Keep Home / Saves / Trips / Map fixed at the bottom.

Do not use a MapKit screenshot, nested cards, oversized copy, a world map, or
multiple primary colors.
