# Research Board

## Current SAV-E Saves

- Relevant behavior: unresolved and confirmed objects share one ticket-and-envelope construction.
- Transferable pattern: keep silhouette and placement stable while semantic color and seal change.
- Why it fits: onboarding teaches the same system users encounter immediately afterward.
- What not to copy: the full queue, tabs, and collection counts are not first-run content.
- Source: `Prototypes/AtlasPostcard/design.md` and approved A+B hybrid.

## Current SAV-E Lifted Saved Postcard

- Relevant behavior: a selected Map Stamp lifts from the pocket while keeping source receipt, photo, postal marks, and confirmation state.
- Transferable pattern: use the lifted postcard as the final proof instead of a generic mini map.
- Why it fits: the final onboarding state previews the real saved-place detail.
- What not to copy: Add to Trip and overflow actions would distract from first-run completion.
- Source: `Prototypes/AtlasPostcard/Reference/approved-ab-hybrid.png` and `design.md`.

## Current SAV-E Opening Composition

- Relevant behavior: Memo sorts overlapping Clue, Review, and Map Stamp tickets into a kraft envelope.
- Transferable pattern: animate one object through the same pocket rather than swapping unrelated cards.
- Why it fits: creates continuity from opening to onboarding to Saves.
- What not to copy: loading status and cycling animation do not belong on decision steps.
- Source: locked `Onboarding and Opening` specification in `design.md`.

## Cross-Reference Synthesis

- Shared pattern: one physical postcard object, one envelope stage, state-specific postal treatment.
- Product-specific opportunity: onboarding can literally demonstrate the object users will later manage in Saves.
- Rejected imitation: floating source chips, checklist rows, and a dashboard-style map confirmation panel.
