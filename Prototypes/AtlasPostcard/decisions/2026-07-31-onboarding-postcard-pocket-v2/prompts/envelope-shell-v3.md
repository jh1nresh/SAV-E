# Onboarding envelope shell v3 asset prompts

The founder-approved `approved-direction.png` is the sole geometry and material
authority. These are section-layer assets only. Live SwiftUI still renders the
state card, copy, controls, source receipt, and accessibility tree.

## Front kraft pocket

Create only the blank front kraft pocket visible across the three approved
phones: tall rounded side shoulders, a recessed concave mouth with a shallow
central point, double stitched mouth and perimeter, warm tactile kraft paper,
subtle fibers, a low soft internal shadow, and the airplane cancellation at
bottom left. Remove Memo, caption, wax ring, cards, letters, rear envelope, UI,
logos, and all text. Use a flat `#00ff00` background for mechanical removal.

## Rear open-envelope liner

Create only the rear layer of the approved opened envelope: broad connected
warm-kraft diagonal wings, narrow coral/cream/sky airmail rails at both outer
edges, and one connected central V fold behind the live letter. The center
above the V stays transparent. Do not create a rectangular border, horizontal
stripe band, vertical bars, detached triangle, closed flap, front pocket,
letter, Memo, UI, logo, postmark, wax seal, or text. Use a flat `#00ff00`
background for mechanical removal.
