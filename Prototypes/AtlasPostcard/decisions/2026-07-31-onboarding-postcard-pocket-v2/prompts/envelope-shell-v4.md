# Onboarding envelope shell v4 extraction prompts

The founder-approved `approved-direction.png` is the strict construction
authority. These are section assets, not alternate designs. Live SwiftUI owns
the letters, copy, controls, Memo poses, and accessibility tree.

## Front kraft pocket

Extract the approved front pocket only: raised side shoulders, shallow inward
mouth, one small centre point/fold, rounded lower corners, thin stitched
perimeter, tactile warm kraft paper, and the airplane cancellation at lower
left. Remove every letter, rear liner, Memo, caption, phone, and UI element.

## Rear side-wing liner

Extract only the two broad kraft diagonal side wings behind the approved
letters. Keep narrow muted coral/cream/sky airmail rails on the outer edges and
one thin stitched diagonal fold on each wing. The entire centre stays
transparent. A large opaque central V, rectangular backing, horizontal stripe
band, detached rail, front pocket, letter, or text is a failed output.

## Mechanical processing

Generate on flat `#00ff00`, remove that background locally, trim, and export
owned 1x/2x/3x RGBA assets. Validate the result composited on the production
cream canvas before promotion.
