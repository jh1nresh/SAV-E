SAV-E Support

Help with your private place memory.

Saving a place

Paste a place link, screenshot, or note into SAV-E. If the place is uncertain, it stays in Review until you confirm it. Confirmed places appear on your private map.

Location access

Location permission is optional. It helps SAV-E show nearby places. You can change access at any time in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security, then Location Services.

Delete your account

In SAV-E, open Passport, choose Delete Account, and confirm. This permanently deletes your SAV-E account and its saved data.

Contact

For help or privacy questions, email support@sav-e.app.
